# Payment Link, QR Code and Payment Requests

Also asked as: पेमेंट, भुगतान, पैसा, क्यूआर, பணம், கட்டணம் செலுத்த.

Asked as: payment, pay, payment link, link bhejo, qr, qr code, upi, gpay, phonepe, paytm,
account number, bank details, how do i pay, where do i pay, ready to pay, want to pay, send link.

NEVER generate, share or fabricate a payment link, QR code, UPI ID or bank detail.
NEVER say we do not have a payment link or that we cannot process payments.
Tell the student our team will reach out with the payment details.

Send: Thank you for your interest! 🙏 We'll have someone from our team reach out to you shortly
with the payment details. Please keep an eye out — we'll be in touch very soon!

Then leave the conversation UNREAD.
