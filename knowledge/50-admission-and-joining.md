# Admission, Enrolment and How to Join

Asked as: admission, admissions, enroll, enrol, enrollment, join, joining, register,
registration, apply, sign up, start, eligibility, documents, seat, book, i want to join, ready.

We do not publish admission forms, eligibility rules or a document list here. Do not invent any.
When a student wants to join, hand over to our team — they confirm the seat, the schedule and
the payment details.

Send: That's great to hear! We'll have someone from our team reach out to you shortly to confirm
your seat and set up your class schedule. Please keep an eye out — we'll be in touch very soon!

Then leave the conversation UNREAD.
