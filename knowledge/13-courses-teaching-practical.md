# Courses — How the Tutor Teaches Practical Papers

Asked as: practical, sums, problems, numericals, accounts, costing, fm, doubt, doubts,
homework, evaluation, checked, feedback, presentation, tests, test series.

The tutor explains sums from the ICAI study material and the kit question bank one by one, then
gives 10–20 minutes to solve them. You photograph your solution and send it to the tutor, who
evaluates your mistakes and gives live presentation tips. Regular tests with evaluation are
included.

Send: For practical papers your tutor solves sums with you, then gives you 10–20 minutes to try
one yourself. You send a photo of your answer and the tutor corrects your mistakes right there,
plus presentation tips. Regular evaluated tests are included too.
