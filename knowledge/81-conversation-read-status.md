# Conversation Read Status — Never Mark As Read

<!-- reference-only: background for the bot, not a reply to send -->
This applies after every escalation: pricing diversion, payment redirect, brochure request, payment success
acknowledgement, payment failure, admission handover, and any query raised with our team. The
conversation must stay UNREAD even when our message is the last one in the thread.

Reason: our executives find pending follow-ups by scanning unread conversations. A conversation
marked read may be missed entirely.
