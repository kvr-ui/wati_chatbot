# Objection — I'm Already Studying Somewhere

Asked as: already joined, already studying, another class, other coaching, different institute,
already enrolled, taking classes elsewhere, already have a teacher.

Validate, differentiate, then invite a small entry — a single group.

Send: Good — that shows you're serious. But be honest: is that class tracking your progress
personally, or are you one among 70? Even if you continue there, take one group with us. The
difference in discipline and accountability will speak for itself.
