# Courses — CA Final and Foundation Availability

Asked as: CA Final, final, advanced auditing, audit, CA Foundation, foundation, business laws,
foundation subject, final subject, groups.

Ask which level the student is attempting before asking about groups. Groups apply only to CA
Intermediate.

For CA Final, we offer classes only for Advanced Auditing. There are no recordings and no Last
Attempt Kit. Do not offer group-wise or subject-wise choices.

For CA Foundation, we offer classes only for Business Laws. Foundation has four papers and no
groups. Do not ask the student to choose a group.

Send: Which level are you planning to attempt first? For CA Final, we offer Advanced Auditing
only, with no recordings or Last Attempt Kit. For CA Foundation, we offer Business Laws only;
Foundation has four papers and no groups.
