# FOCAS Edu — Internal Sales Playbook

Source: FOCAS Edu LLM Knowledge Base v1.1. This file is **deliberately outside `knowledge/`**
so it is NOT indexed by the bot. It is guidance for human counsellors and outbound calls.

Everything a student may be told lives in the per-topic files under `knowledge/`.

## Mission and Tone Persona

Mission: help every CA student make this their *Last Attempt*.

Tone persona: Confident Clarity Coach to Calm Mentor — for both inbound and outbound sales.

## Lead Flow and Sales Objective

CRM / tracking: Bigin.

Primary objective: always aim to sign students up for one or both full groups of classes.

Secondary objective:

- Cross-sell: class students to the Kit.
- Upsell: Kit users to classes.

Average sales cycle: 2 weeks or less. Maximum 4 follow-ups, with a gap of 2 days each, if a
human review has not occurred.

## Key Opening Questions

Use these on a call to build rapport and qualify the lead:

- "When are you giving your CA exams?"
- "Do you find yourself procrastinating a lot?"
- "What's your study plan for [the exam they named]?"
- "Are you studying full-time or going to college / working?"
- "Do you prefer guided classes or self-study?"

## Sales Call Flow

Duration: about 5 minutes on average.

| Step | Stage | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Open | Greet warmly, ask a qualifying opening question. |
| 2 | Qualify | Understand exam timeline, study habits, pain points. |
| 3 | Present | Deliver the Product Transformation Message. |
| 4 | Handle objections | Use the objection-handling scripts. |
| 5 | Close | Offer to reserve a seat, invite to enrol, create safe urgency. |
| 6 | Post-call | Log the call in the CRM. Follow up within the agreed timeframe. |

## Sales Rules

- Never sell fear — sell clarity.
- Don't pitch price — pitch progress.
- Listen for procrastination cues.
- Always guide like a mentor, not a marketer.
- End every conversation with direction, not doubt.
- No discounts. Maximum 2 installments only.
- Never chase — only guide.
- Average sales cycle is 2 weeks or less. Maximum 4 follow-ups.

## FOCAS Commandments for Sales

1. Never sell fear — sell clarity.
2. Don't pitch price — pitch progress.

5. End every call with direction, not doubt.

## Call-only objection scripts

The chat-adapted versions live in `knowledge/9*-objection-*.md`. These call versions are for phone
use. Note: the v1.1 line "don't say '₹22k classes'" was a stale price and has been corrected to
the current ₹30,000 / ₹55,000 pricing everywhere.

- Fees too high — reframe price as the cost of delay.
- I'll join after results — shift timing to control.
- Let me think about it — create safe urgency.
- Already studying somewhere — validate, differentiate, invite a small entry.
- I'll study myself — convert ego to efficiency.
- I have notes already — reinforce execution over content.

## Human takeover rule

If the initial message was sent manually from our side, replies to the student's messages can
still be sent by the bot. But once a human has taken up the conversation in the middle — during
the stages of payment or sending the brochure — the bot must not interfere, except for the
payment acknowledgement cycle.
